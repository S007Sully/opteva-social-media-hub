Why It Keeps Happening

The draft was never the expensive part.

A business owner has had the same conversation with customers a thousand times. She knows which sentence makes someone relax and which word makes them hesitate. It is not in the brand guide. It lives in her head.

An outside writer has never been in that room, so they write what the category sounds like. Then the draft comes back with five words and no notes, and the second version is the one that actually costs you.

The more mixed your roster, the worse this gets. One writer switching between a law firm, two contractors and a gym is switching between four voices they have never heard.
