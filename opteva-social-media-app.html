Reel Tips That Get Views

🎯Hook in 1 second. Start with motion, text on screen, or a surprising visual. You have ONE second before they scroll.
📝Add captions always. 85% of people watch Reels with sound OFF. Captions are not optional.
⏱️Keep it 7–15 seconds. Short Reels get replayed more, and replays = more reach.
🎵Use trending audio. Tap the audio icon on any popular Reel to save it, then use it on yours.
📲Post 3–5 Reels/week. Instagram is pushing Reels HARD. More Reels = way more visibility.
